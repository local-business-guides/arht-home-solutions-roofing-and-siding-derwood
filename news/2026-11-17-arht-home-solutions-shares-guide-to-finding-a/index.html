Readers in Rockville will find practical points to consider when looking for a local roofing contractor. The guide covers checking that a contractor is licensed, confirming which home services are offered and asking how storm damage insurance claims are handled. These questions can help homeowners compare providers and understand what to ask before arranging work.

The information may be useful when a homeowner is planning a roof replacement or dealing with storm damage and wants to learn about local options.
